---
title: The Power of Listening
date: 2026-09-28
author: Sam Rivera
summary: A reflection on why the most compassionate thing we can do is often just to listen.
---

Sometimes we rush to fix things. We offer advice, solutions, and plans. But often, the people around us simply want to be heard.

> "The first duty of love is to listen." — Paul Tillich

At our last reflection circle, we practiced listening without interrupting for five full minutes. It was harder than it sounds — and more meaningful than any of us expected.

Try it this week: ask someone how they're really doing, and then just listen.
