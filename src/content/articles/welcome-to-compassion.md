Welcome! We're so glad you're here.

Compassion began with a simple idea: that small, consistent acts of kindness can change a community. This year we're focusing on three things — showing up for our neighbors, learning from one another, and making service a habit rather than an event.

## What's ahead

- Monthly service days across the community
- Reflection circles after each project
- A spring fundraiser supporting local families

Thank you for being part of the story.
